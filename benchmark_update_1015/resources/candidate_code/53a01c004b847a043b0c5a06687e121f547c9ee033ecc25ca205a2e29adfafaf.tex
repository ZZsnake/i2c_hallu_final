A vintage wedding decor rental business conducted a study to analyze the effect of different vintage decor styles on customer satisfaction ratings. They tested four styles: Rustic Farmhouse, Victorian Elegance, Art Deco Glam, and Bohemian Chic. Each style was used in 5 different weddings, and customers rated their satisfaction on a scale from 1 to 10. The following data was collected:

\vspace{0.5em}
\begin{tabular}{ll}
Rustic Farmhouse: & 8, 7, 9, 8, 7 \\
Victorian Elegance: & 9, 8, 10, 9, 8 \\
Art Deco Glam: & 7, 6, 8, 7, 6 \\
Bohemian Chic: & 8, 9, 7, 8, 8
\end{tabular}
\vspace{0.5em}

Conduct a one-way ANOVA at a significance level of $\alpha = 0.05$ to determine if there is a significant difference in customer satisfaction ratings among the different vintage decor styles. What is the F-statistic for this analysis?
