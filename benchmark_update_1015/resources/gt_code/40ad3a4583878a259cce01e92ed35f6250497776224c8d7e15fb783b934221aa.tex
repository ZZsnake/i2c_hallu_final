\begin{multicols}{2}

\section*{\faLightbulb\ Introduction}
BrightStay Hostel, located in Barcelona, Spain, faced rising energy costs and increased competition. To address these challenges, the hostel implemented energy efficiency initiatives to lower operational costs and attract eco-conscious travelers.

\section*{\faSolarPanel\ Solar Panel Installation}
\textbf{Investment:} €60,000 for a 50 kW system
\begin{itemize}
\item Covers 70\% of annual electricity needs
\item 30\% reduction in electricity bills
\item €15,000 annual savings
\item 40 metric tons CO2 reduction per year
\end{itemize}

\section*{\faLightbulb\ LED Lighting Retrofit}
\textbf{Investment:} €10,000
\begin{itemize}
\item 50\% reduction in lighting energy consumption
\item €4,000 annual savings
\item Longer bulb lifespan, reduced maintenance
\end{itemize}

\section*{\faTemperatureHigh\ Smart Thermostats}
\textbf{Investment:} €8,000
\begin{itemize}
\item 20\% reduction in HVAC energy usage
\item €3,000 annual savings
\item Improved guest comfort and satisfaction
\end{itemize}

\section*{\faChartLine\ Results}
\textbf{Total Annual Savings:} €22,000
\begin{itemize}
\item Total investment: €78,000
\item Payback period: 3.5 years
\item 10\% increase in bookings due to eco-friendly reputation
\end{itemize}

\section*{\faCheckCircle\ Conclusion}
BrightStay Hostel's strategic investments in energy efficiency have resulted in significant cost savings, improved sustainability, and increased guest satisfaction. This case study demonstrates that environmentally responsible choices can be economically beneficial for the hospitality industry.
